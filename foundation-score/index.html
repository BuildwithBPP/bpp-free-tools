How the Foundation Readiness Score works

The Foundation Readiness Score checks whether a new business has its basics in place before it pushes for growth. It's built for owners who are just getting started or still setting up. It asks 10 questions, 2 for each of 5 foundations, and takes about 2 minutes.

The 5 foundations

  • Legal & Money Basics. Is the business set up right, with its money kept apart from yours?
  • Brand & Presence. Can a customer see who you are and find you online?
  • Offer & Pricing. Is it clear what you sell, and is the price based on real costs?
  • First-Clients Engine. Do you know where customers will come from, and does every lead get followed up?
  • Simple Operations. Do you deliver the same way every time, and do you know your numbers?

The 10 questions

Each question has five answers, from the weakest (1) to the strongest (5).

  1. Legal & Money Is your business legally set up: entity registered, EIN, licenses you need? From “Nothing yet” to “Fully set up.”
  2. Legal & Money Are business finances fully separated from personal, with bookkeeping that's up to date? From “All mixed together” to “Separated and current.”
  3. Brand & Presence Do you have a name, logo, and look you'd put in front of any customer? From “Still deciding” to “Complete and consistent.”
  4. Brand & Presence Can a stranger find you online and see what you offer (website, Google profile)? From “Can't find us” to “Strong presence, reviews.”
  5. Offer & Pricing Can you explain what you sell, who it's for, and what it costs in two sentences? From “Still figuring it out” to “Crystal clear.”
  6. Offer & Pricing Is your pricing based on real costs and margin, not a guess or a competitor's number? From “Pure guess” to “Priced with real margin math.”
  7. First Clients Do you know exactly where your next 5 customers will come from? From “No idea” to “Repeatable channel proven.”
  8. First Clients Do you have a way to capture and follow up with every person who shows interest? From “Nothing” to “System that never drops one.”
  9. Operations When you win a customer, is there a repeatable way you deliver the work? From “Reinvent it each time” to “Documented and repeatable.”
  10. Operations Do you know your numbers each month: what came in, what went out, what's left? From “Never look” to “Clear monthly picture.”

How your score is calculated

Each answer is worth 1 to 5 points. Each foundation has 2 questions, so it scores between 2 and 10 points.

Foundation percent = (that foundation's points − 2) ÷ 8
Foundation Readiness Score = the average of your 5 foundation percents, rounded to a whole number

Two strongest answers make a foundation 100%. Two weakest answers make it 0%. Every foundation counts the same in the average. Higher is better here.

A foundation under 70% counts as a gap. Your result names up to two gaps, lowest first. If two gaps tie, they follow build order: Legal & Money Basics, then Offer & Pricing, then Brand & Presence, then First-Clients Engine, then Simple Operations. The offer comes before the brand because the brand has to describe the offer.

How to read your result

  • 70% or higher: Ready to Build. Your foundation is solid. The next question is whether the business can grow without running on you, so the result points you to the Owner-Dependency Scorecard.
  • 40% to 69%: Almost Ready. You've done real work, but a few gaps need closing before you spend money on growth.
  • Under 40%: Foundation First. Spending on growth now would leak. Work the foundations in order, starting with your biggest gap.

Each foundation also gets a bar. Green is 70% or better, amber is 40% to 69%, and red is under 40%. Under the bars, the result names what to close first. For example, if Offer & Pricing is the gap, the fix is locking one clear offer with pricing built on real margin math. Common questions

Who is this score for?

Owners who are just getting started or still setting up. If your business has been running a while and you're stuck in the day-to-day, take the Owner-Dependency Scorecard instead.

Why is a higher score better here?

This score measures how ready your foundation is, so more is better. The Owner-Dependency Scorecard works the same way: its Run-Without-You Score is out of 100, and higher means the business runs better without you.

Why does every foundation count the same?

Each foundation has 2 questions, and the score is the plain average of the 5. No single area can carry the score on its own.

What should I fix first?

Start with the first gap your result names. Ties already follow the build order, so your gaps come out in the order to work them.

Do I need to give my email?

Yes, for this score. We ask for your name and email, then show your score on the page and email you a copy.
